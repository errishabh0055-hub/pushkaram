Welcome to Pushkaram

Where Heritage Meets Tranquil Comfort

A serene desert retreat nestled near the holy town of Pushkar, designed for families, leisure travelers, and pilgrimage groups.

Pushkaram Resort is situated away from crowded city lanes while remaining comfortably accessible to the sacred Brahma Temple, holy Pushkar Sarovar (Lake), and annual Pushkar Mela grounds.

Featuring authentic carved stone jharokhas, an expansive outdoor swimming pool with heritage arches, banquet facilities for intimate celebrations, and verdant open lawns, our resort is dedicated to genuine warmth and heartfelt Rajasthani hospitality.
